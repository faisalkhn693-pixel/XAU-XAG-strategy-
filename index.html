Strategy Rules

±4 Eligibility: A metal becomes eligible once it reaches +4 or −4.
Normal Trade: +4 or −4.
Good Trade: +5 / +6 or −5 / −6.
XAU / XAG Opposite: Only judge them opposite when BOTH metals have reached the ±4 eligibility threshold.
Example: XAU +3 and XAG −5 are NOT considered opposite because XAU is not yet eligible.
Same Direction: If both eligible metals have the same direction, prefer the stronger score and cleaner location.
XAG Dominance: When the situation is otherwise equal, XAG is dominant.
Entry Priority: Daily VWAP = best option. VAL / VAH (Daily / Weekly) = second best.
VAL / VAH: VAL or VAH alone is NOT an entry. It must have at least one additional confluence such as Band 1 or POC.
Sweep: Liquidity sweep is an additional sniper-entry confirmation. It is not required.
Monday: Use previous week's POC / VAH / VAL.
Pending Trade: Use a pending order at the best-defined entry location.
Stop Loss: Try to place SL below / above a major structure.
Target: Best TP = PDH or PDL.
Preferred RR: 1:3.
Trial: Record XAU and XAG from the same entry to compare which performs better.
