Strategy Rules

Eligibility: +4 or higher = bullish eligible. −4 or lower = bearish eligible.
Opposite: XAU and XAG are only considered opposite when BOTH have reached ±4 and their directions disagree.
Both Bullish: If XAU is +4 or higher AND XAG is +4 or higher → TAKE ENTRY FROM NEAREST ENTRY POINT.
Both Bearish: If XAU is −4 or lower AND XAG is −4 or lower → TAKE ENTRY FROM NEAREST ENTRY POINT.
XAG Dominant: When conditions are otherwise equal, XAG is dominant.
Normal Trade: ±4.
Good Trade: ±5 / ±6.
Score: Positive and negative points are counted separately. They are never combined.
Entry Priority: Daily VWAP is the best location. Daily / Weekly VAH or VAL is second best.
VAH / VAL: VAH or VAL alone is not an entry. One additional confluence is required.
Sweep: Liquidity sweep is additional confirmation / sniper entry, not mandatory.
Monday: Use previous week's POC / VAH / VAL.
Target: PDH / PDL.
RR: 1:3.
